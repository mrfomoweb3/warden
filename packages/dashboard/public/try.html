Try Warden on a live agent

Connect a Sui wallet, name your agent and give Warden a rule in plain English. It checks your rule against the real payments of one of our demo agents, and alerts you in Telegram when one breaks it.

Shadow mode. Your rules run on Warden's real policy engine and real testnet payments, but only the vault's owner can pause the real agents. Signing in costs nothing and moves no funds. Your rules are saved to Walrus Memory.
