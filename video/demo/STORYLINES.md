# Warden demo film: three storylines

The one thing: **your AI agents can spend on their own; Warden watches every payment, stops the bad ones on-chain, and asks less as it learns.**
Spine: one real agent (`rogue`) and one real payment pattern (the same search API, 30 times in 10 minutes) travel from the hook to the payoff.
All facts are from the real testnet run (see README "Deployment").

## A. The 2 a.m. loop (recommended)

- **Device:** the bug itself, as a moment in someone's night. **Form:** object-to-object chain: every shot ends on the thing that opens the next.
- **What you see and feel:** a payment counter ticks up while a wallet drains, nobody awake; then a Telegram ⛔ lands, the vault slams shut, and the camera pulls back to show everything Warden did and remembered.
- **First 2 seconds:** frame 0 is the real terminal line `[rogue] paid …/search?vendor=search (tx …)` repeating, a big counter beside it hits 28, 29, 30; a phone buzzes at the edge of frame.
- **Beats:** hook (loop counter) → tension ("alerts tell you after the money moved") → reveal (WARDEN title out of the phone notification) → proof: type a rule in English → `set_caps` tx; a ❓ question with its 💬 explanation → tap Always allow → rule learned; the ⛔ pause → vault refuses 66 tries → Suiscan tx → payoff (dashboard: questions per day falling) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the brand's own world: the sky photo and grass from the site, Instrument Serif titles, white rounded cards, olive/maroon accents; the terminal is the one dark surface.
- **Sound:** calm narrator (HeyGen voice), a low pulse that speeds with the counter and cuts dead on the pause, soft UI clicks and a phone buzz.

## B. One payment's journey

- **Device:** one object across apps. **Form:** one continuous take, never cutting.
- **What you see and feel:** we ride a single $0.002 payment from the agent through the 402, the vault, Tatum, Warden, Telegram and into Walrus Memory, then out again in Claude Code answering "why was rogue paused?". It feels like a guided tour of a living system.
- **First 2 seconds:** a coin-sized "0.002 SUI" token drops out of the agent's request line and the camera follows it down into the vault.
- **Beats:** hook (the payment leaves) → tension (it's the 30th to the same API) → reveal (Warden catches it) → proof: Tatum feed → rule check → on-chain pause → Telegram alert → Walrus memory record → payoff (Claude Code recalls it on another machine) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the sky and grass world as one long landscape the camera travels across; each system is a stop on the hill.
- **Sound:** narrator, a travelling music bed with a hit at each stop.

## C. Week one, week three (the bold one)

- **Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen collapsing to one.
- **What you see and feel:** two phones side by side: week one buzzes with question after question; week three stays quiet while the dashboard counts payments handled silently. The noisy side collapses away and the calm one fills the frame.
- **First 2 seconds:** two identical phones; the left one lights up three times in a row, the right one doesn't.
- **Beats:** hook (noisy vs quiet) → tension (week one: every new vendor is a question) → reveal (your replies become rules) → proof: Always allow → generalised rule → the rogue pause still happens in week three → payoff (the real questions-per-day chart falling) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the sky world with two phones on the grass; the chart is drawn from real data.
- **Sound:** narrator; a busy rhythm on the left that thins out to one sustained note on the right.
- **Depends on:** the multi-day run producing a real falling curve first (so it can't be finished before your Telegram answers accumulate).
